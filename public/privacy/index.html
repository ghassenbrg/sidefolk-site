Privacy

Sidefolk is local first. Everything it keeps is in one folder on your Mac. There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporter.

What stays on your Mac

Your data in one folder, readable only by you. Logs for 7 days, with secrets taken out. API keys in your Keychain. Conversations are kept 7 days (you choose how long) and can be deleted from History. Sidefolk never reads your screen or other apps' windows; it knows the name of the app in front.

Your data, your choice

Settings › Trust exports everything as JSON or deletes everything, Keychain items included.

This website

This site has no accounts, cookies, analytics or trackers, and it loads nothing from other services. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service running.
